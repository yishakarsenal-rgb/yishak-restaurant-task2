Our Story

A heritage of traditional Ethiopian food, crafted with care.

Rooted in the timeless traditions of Ethiopian culinary arts, Yishak Restaurant is dedicated to preserving the authentic flavors of our heritage. We bring you the definitive traditional Ethiopian food experience—where rich berbere spices, perfectly seasoned meats, and slow-simmered stews meet to create an unforgettable communal feast shared over freshly baked injera.

  • ✓ Authentic traditional Ethiopian main dishes
  • ✓ Classic holiday favorites and everyday staples
  • ✓ Freshly prepared injera served with every meal
  • ✓ Rich, traditional vegetarian and fasting platters
